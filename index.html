Architecture

How a question becomes a grounded answer

No live scraping happens at question time — that's neither reliable nor fast. Instead, sources are added ahead of time through an admin tool, and every answer is limited to what's actually been added.

(a)

Retrieve

The question is matched against stored excerpts from eCFR, the DEA, and state boards of pharmacy, ranked by how closely each excerpt relates to what's being asked.

(b)

Ground

Only the best-matching excerpts — never the open web, never the model's own memory — are handed to Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite with an explicit instruction to answer from them alone, or say plainly that nothing loaded so far covers the question.

(c)

Cite

Every answer returns citations tied to a specific section — not just "somewhere in this document" — and clicking one opens the exact excerpt with the relevant sentence highlighted, plus a link that highlights that same passage on the live source page.

(d)

Flag conflicts

Every excerpt is tagged with its jurisdiction. When a federal rule and a state rule — or two states — genuinely disagree on the same question, the answer calls it out explicitly and says which one is more restrictive, instead of silently picking one.
